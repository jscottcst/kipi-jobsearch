## Active Objections

### "You don't have Solutions Engineering experience"
- **Persona:** Hiring manager, SE team lead
- **Real concern:** Will you need 6+ months to get productive? Can you run a technical demo on day one?
- **Response:** "My role has always been the technical translator — I own the implementation, I explain it to stakeholders, and I troubleshoot when vendors fall short. I've done the SE job from the buyer side. I know what good looks like because I've evaluated it."
- **Proof needed:** A story about owning a vendor evaluation or running an internal implementation that had an external-facing component
- **What NOT to say:** "I'm a fast learner" — sounds like you're admitting the gap instead of reframing it
- **Source:** Anticipated from career pivot context - 2026-06-30 {{ANTICIPATED}}

### "Your background is internal IT, not customer-facing"
- **Persona:** SE hiring manager, recruiter screening for SE roles
- **Real concern:** SE requires presenting to prospects, handling live objections, and building rapport under pressure — can you do that without prior reps?
- **Response:** "Continental Stock Transfer supports external clients — transfer agents, issuers, shareholders. I've interfaced with those stakeholders on technical issues and implementations. That's not the same as a quota-carrying SE role, but I'm not starting from zero on external communication."
- **Proof needed:** Specific example of client-facing technical work — even internal clients count if the dynamic was adversarial or high-stakes
- **What NOT to say:** "I'm great with people" — vague and unverifiable
- **Source:** Anticipated - 2026-06-30 {{ANTICIPATED}}

### "Do you have any experience with technical sales or demos?"
- **Persona:** Recruiter, SE manager at a SaaS/fintech vendor
- **Real concern:** SE candidates with zero demo experience often freeze or over-explain in front of prospects
- **Response:** "I've been on the other side of hundreds of vendor demos. I know what loses a room — feature dumps, jargon, no tie-back to pain. I'd rather show you how I'd run a demo than describe it."
- **Proof needed:** Willingness to do a mock demo in the interview loop — request it proactively
- **What NOT to say:** "I haven't done demos but I'm confident I could" — confidence without evidence
- **Source:** Anticipated - 2026-06-30 {{ANTICIPATED}}

### "Why are you leaving financial services?"
- **Persona:** Any interviewer
- **Real concern:** Are you running from something? Will you leave again in 6 months?
- **Response:** "I'm not leaving financial services — I'm moving toward selling into it. My 7 years inside a financial services firm is exactly what makes me valuable to a fintech vendor. I know the compliance constraints, the procurement process, and the IT landscape their customers operate in."
- **Proof needed:** Research on the target company's FS client base — name a specific customer segment they sell to
- **What NOT to say:** Anything negative about Continental Stock Transfer
- **Source:** Anticipated - 2026-06-30 {{ANTICIPATED}}

### "You're overqualified for this IT Engineer role"
- **Persona:** Recruiter or hiring manager on purely technical roles
- **Real concern:** Will you leave the moment something better comes along?
- **Response:** "I'm targeting a range of roles deliberately. I want to find the right fit — team, scope, growth — not just the right title. Tell me more about what the growth path looks like here."
- **Proof needed:** Genuine interest signals — questions about the team, not just the salary
- **What NOT to say:** "I just need a job" — kills leverage
- **Source:** Anticipated - 2026-06-30 {{ANTICIPATED}}
