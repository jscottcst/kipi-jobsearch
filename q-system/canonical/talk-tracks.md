## Core Positioning <!-- pin -->

### One-liner
> Systems engineer with financial services depth, moving into roles where that domain knowledge is a weapon — Solutions Engineering, IT Engineering, or technical roles at fintech vendors.

### Primary framing
> "I've been the buyer. Now I want to be the builder on the other side of the table."

### Category
> Technical generalist with FS domain depth — not a pure developer, not a pure IT ops person. Sits at the intersection of infrastructure, process, and business context.

## By Audience

### For Recruiters (phone screen)

#### 30-second intro
- **Audience:** Recruiter cold screen or LinkedIn message reply
- **Context:** First 60 seconds of any call — before they ask questions
- **The line:** "I'm a systems engineer at Continental Stock Transfer — financial services IT. I've spent 7 years owning infrastructure, vendor relationships, and implementations. I'm targeting Solutions Engineer roles at fintech vendors and Systems/IT Engineer roles in FS more broadly. My edge is that I know the environment your clients operate in."
- **Why it works:** Establishes domain credibility immediately. Signals the SE pivot with a clear rationale, not just a desire.
- **Source:** Built from profile - 2026-06-30

#### Handling "why are you looking?"
- **Audience:** Recruiter
- **Context:** Second question on every screen
- **The line:** "I've built a lot in my current role and I'm ready for a bigger surface area — more complex environments, more stakeholder exposure, or a vendor-side role where I can put my FS knowledge to work externally."
- **Why it works:** Implies growth motivation, not flight motivation. Avoids badmouthing current employer.
- **Source:** Built from profile - 2026-06-30

### For SE Hiring Managers

#### The career pivot narrative
- **Audience:** SE team lead or hiring manager interviewing for Solutions Engineer
- **Context:** "Tell me about yourself" or "Walk me through your background"
- **The line:** "I've spent my career on the buy side of enterprise technology. I evaluate vendors, run implementations, and translate between what IT needs and what the business wants. I've been in every meeting that a good SE should be running — I just wasn't the SE. I want to flip the table."
- **Why it works:** Reframes lack of SE title as an asset (buyer perspective). "Flip the table" is memorable and signals self-awareness.
- **Source:** Built from profile - 2026-06-30

#### Domain expertise pitch for fintech SE roles
- **Audience:** Hiring manager at a fintech vendor selling into financial services
- **Context:** When they ask "do you know our market?"
- **The line:** "Your customers are dealing with transfer agent workflows, shareholder data management, regulatory reporting, and compliance-driven procurement. I've lived inside that environment. I know why their IT teams are slow to buy and what it takes to get a deal through their security review."
- **Why it works:** Specific pain points, not generic "I know finance." Shows the candidate has intel the SE team would pay for.
- **Source:** Built from profile - 2026-06-30

### For Networking / Informational Interviews

#### Asking for a conversation
- **Audience:** Former colleague, LinkedIn connection, 2nd-degree contact
- **Context:** DM or email requesting 20 minutes
- **The line:** "I'm exploring Solutions Engineering roles in fintech. You've been in that world — I'd value 20 minutes to understand what the day-to-day actually looks like and whether my background would translate. No ask beyond the conversation."
- **Why it works:** Low stakes, specific topic, explicit no-ask. Easiest yes they'll give.
- **Source:** Built from profile - 2026-06-30
