# Target Verticals

> Role types and industries to pursue, ranked by priority. Agents use this to score and filter opportunities.

## Active Verticals

### Vertical: Solutions Engineer at Fintech Vendor <!-- pin -->
- **Target employer:** SaaS or services company selling to banks, broker-dealers, transfer agents, wealth management firms
- **Why it fits:** Jordan's FS domain knowledge is a differentiator for fintech SE teams - shortens ramp, adds credibility in prospect conversations
- **Role signals to look for:** "SE", "Sales Engineer", "Technical Account Manager", "Pre-Sales Engineer", "Solutions Consultant"
- **Key differentiator to lead with:** Buyer-side perspective - knows how FS IT teams evaluate and procure
- **Common gaps to address:** No prior SE title, limited customer-facing reps
- **Status:** Active - highest priority

### Vertical: Systems Engineer in Financial Services <!-- pin -->
- **Target employer:** Banks, broker-dealers, transfer agents, asset managers, insurance, compliance tech
- **Why it fits:** Direct extension of current role and domain - lowest friction to land
- **Role signals to look for:** "Systems Engineer", "Infrastructure Engineer", "IT Systems Engineer", "Enterprise Systems"
- **Key differentiator to lead with:** 7 years hands-on in a regulated FS environment
- **Common gaps to address:** May be seen as lateral rather than growth
- **Status:** Active - secondary priority

### Vertical: IT Engineer (Fintech or Tech-Adjacent) <!-- pin -->
- **Target employer:** Mid-to-large tech companies, SaaS companies with mature IT orgs, fintech platforms
- **Why it fits:** Broadens the search without abandoning technical depth
- **Role signals to look for:** "IT Engineer", "IT Systems Administrator", "Systems Administrator", "Corporate IT Engineer"
- **Key differentiator to lead with:** Experience in a compliance-heavy, operationally complex environment
- **Common gaps to address:** Pure tech companies may not value FS domain depth
- **Status:** Active - tertiary priority
