# Engagement Playbook

> Rules for LinkedIn outreach, DMs, comments, and follow-up during the job search.

## Core Rules <!-- pin -->

1. **Never lead with the ask** - build context first, then make one specific request
2. **Max 3-4 sentences** per comment or DM opening
3. **1 outreach per person per week** unless they reply
4. **Every touchpoint logged** to relationships.md (name, date, type, next action)
5. **Tone adapted to relationship type:**
   - Recruiter: direct, efficient, no fluff
   - Hiring manager: peer-level, domain-informed, curious
   - Peer/practitioner: collegial, shared experience
   - Warm connection: personal context first, ask second

## Comment Styles

### Insight Style
Add a non-obvious observation grounded in your FS experience. Connect their post to the financial services environment or operational reality they may not have seen firsthand.

### Peer Style
Validate from shared experience. "I ran into this exact issue on the buy side at a transfer agent..." - puts you in the room without pitching.

### Question Style
Ask a genuine question that shows you read the post carefully. Not "great insight!" but a specific follow-up that advances the conversation and signals your domain knowledge.

## DM Rules <!-- pin -->

- Start with "I" not their name (never "Hey Jordan, love your work...")
- One ask OR one question. Never both in the same message.
- No "hope you're well" or "hope this finds you"
- Reference something specific - their post, their company, their role
- Get to the point in 2-3 sentences
- Sign off with just your first name

## Outreach Templates by Context

### Recruiter cold contact
"I saw you work on [SE / fintech / FS tech] roles. I'm a systems engineer at Continental Stock Transfer with 7 years of FS IT experience, actively looking at SE and systems roles in fintech. Worth a quick call?"

### Hiring manager at target company
"I've been following [Company]'s work on [specific thing]. I'm exploring SE roles and my background is on the buy side of financial services IT - I know the environment your customers operate in. Open to a conversation?"

### Peer / warm intro ask
"I'm making a move toward Solutions Engineering in fintech. You've been in that world - I'd value 20 minutes to hear what the SE day-to-day actually looks like. No ask beyond the conversation."

## Follow-Up Cadences

### Post-application (no recruiter contact yet)
- Day 0: Apply
- Day 7: LinkedIn message to recruiter or HR contact at company
- Day 14: One follow-up email if no response
- Day 21: Move to Cooling if still no response

### Post-recruiter screen
- Same day: send thank-you email, reference one specific thing from the call
- Day 5: Follow up if no next steps communicated
- Day 10: Final follow-up, then move to Cooling

### Post-interview (any round)
- Same day: thank-you email to each interviewer, one specific callback per person
- Day 5: Check in with recruiter if no feedback
- Day 10: Final check-in, then move to Cooling

## Engagement Tracking

Log every touchpoint in relationships.md with:
- Person name + title + company
- Platform (LinkedIn / email / phone)
- Type (application / DM / comment / call / interview)
- One-line content summary
- Date
- Next action + due date
