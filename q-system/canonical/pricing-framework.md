## Negotiation Rules <!-- pin -->

- Never give a number first on a recruiter screen - deflect with "I'm focused on finding the right fit, happy to discuss comp once we're both interested"
- Always negotiate - first offer is rarely final, especially at Series B+ companies
- Counter at the top of the range, not the middle
- Get the full offer in writing before giving notice
- Equity: ask for vesting schedule and strike price; for early-stage companies, assume illiquid
